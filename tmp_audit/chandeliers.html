The Collection

Handcrafted Crystal Chandeliers

Our chandeliers are the centrepiece of every home we help design — hand-blown crystal drops, hand-cut glass arms, and finishes that range from ruby-red antique brass to aged copper and gilt gold. Every piece is assembled in Firozabad, where our karigars have been shaping decorative glass since 1981. Choose from soaring multi-tier chandeliers for double-height foyers and banquet halls, sculptural single-tier statement pieces for dining rooms, or delicate three-arm designs sized for Indian apartments. Popular finishes include gilt brass, antique bronze, matte black and polished nickel; popular glass profiles include crystal prism, cut-glass diamond, scallop crown and pinecone drop. Every chandelier is available for pre-order — production and dispatch timelines are confirmed after enquiry.
