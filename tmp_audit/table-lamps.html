The Collection

Table Lamps for Consoles, Studies & Bedsides

Table lamps for the Indian home need to do more than light a corner — they need to hold their own on a console, a study desk or a bedside cabinet. This collection features hand-etched glass shades, hand-turned solid brass bases, cut-glass hurricanes and traditional oil-lamp silhouettes reimagined for modern homes. Every piece is assembled in our Firozabad workshop, where the glasswork is done in-house. Popular styles include the ruby-red etched hurricane, the frosted-floral bedside lamp and the classic tulip-shade reading light. Finishes range from antique brass to aged bronze, gilt gold and polished nickel. All table lamps are available for pre-order — we confirm shade colour, base finish and switch type after your enquiry so the piece matches your interior perfectly.
