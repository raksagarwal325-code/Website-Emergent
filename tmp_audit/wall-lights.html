Wall Lights & Decorative Sconces

Wall lights and sconces add warmth to hallways, dressing tables, staircases and reading nooks without occupying floor space. Our collection spans classic Anglo-Indian wall brackets in cut-glass and gilt brass, contemporary etched glass tulips, mid-century flute sconces and ornate diamond-cut luster shades. Every piece is hand-assembled in Firozabad by our karigars, who specialise in the delicate finish work that distinguishes premium wall lights from mass-market fittings. Popular pairings include matched wall-and-ceiling sets for dining rooms and coordinated multi-light installations for staircases. Available in single-arm, twin-arm and cluster configurations. All wall lights are available for pre-order — production and dispatch timelines are confirmed after enquiry, and installation guidance is provided over WhatsApp.
