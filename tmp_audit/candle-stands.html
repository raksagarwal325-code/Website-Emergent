The Collection

Candle Stands, Hurricane Covers & Candelabras

Candle stands and hurricane covers are the finishing touch on any luxury dining table, mantelpiece, temple altar or wedding-ceremony setup. Our collection features hand-cut crystal hurricane shades, hand-etched frosted-glass columns and classic three-arm brass candelabras finished in gilt gold and antique bronze. Every piece is produced in Firozabad using traditional glasswork techniques dating back to 1981 — the same techniques that go into our chandeliers and hanging lights. Popular pairings include matched candle-stand-and-hurricane sets for banquets and hotel restaurants, and single-piece heritage stands for private homes and temples. Available in single, double, triple and multi-tier configurations. All candle stands are available for pre-order — production and dispatch timelines are confirmed after enquiry.
