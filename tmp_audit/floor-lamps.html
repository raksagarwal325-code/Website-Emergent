Floor Lamps & Statement Column Lights

Floor lamps in the Samrat Glass Emporium collection are traditional Indian statement pieces — multi-tier column lamps, Ottoman-inspired lantern trees and heritage tall-brass designs commonly seen in luxury hotels, private villas and premium showrooms. Each floor lamp is hand-assembled in Firozabad on a solid brass or cast-iron base for stability, with hand-blown or hand-etched glass shades sourced from the same atelier that produces our chandeliers. Popular styles include the three-tier mosaic globe lamp, the Ottoman lantern tree with hand-painted glass and the classic single-shade reading lamp. Finishes include antique brass, aged bronze and polished gold. All floor lamps are available for pre-order — production and dispatch timelines are confirmed after enquiry.
