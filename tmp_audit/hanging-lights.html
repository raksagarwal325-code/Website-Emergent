Hanging Lights & Decorative Pendants

Hanging lights are the workhorse of luxury Indian lighting — from single-pendant clusters over kitchen islands to bell-shaped brass lanterns above staircase landings and dining tables. Each fixture in this collection is hand-assembled in our Firozabad atelier using traditional glass-blowing techniques passed down through three generations. Choose amber-tinted hurricane pendants for restaurants and lounges, smoked-glass domes for modern homes, hand-etched patterns for temples and heritage villas, or minimal clear-glass bells for contemporary flats. Chain, rod and cable suspensions are available in gilt brass, antique bronze, brushed nickel and matte black. Every hanging light is available for pre-order — we finalise dimensions, cord length and voltage after your enquiry so the piece fits your ceiling and load-bearing spec exactly.
